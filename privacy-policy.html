Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 20, 2026

At Fix My Roof CT, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data when you use our website to request roofing quotes.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through our secure web forms, which includes your Full Name, Phone Number, Email Address, and details regarding your roofing needs.

2. How We Use Your Information

Fix My Roof CT is a lead generation matching service. We use the information you provide exclusively to connect you with independent, licensed local roofing contractors who can service your request. By submitting our form, you consent to having your contact information securely transmitted to these specific local professionals so they can provide your free quote.

3. SMS/Text Messaging Consent & Twilio 10DLC Compliance

By checking the SMS opt-in box on our form, you provide express written consent to receive communications via SMS text messages, phone calls, and emails from Fix My Roof CT and our specific network of local roofing professionals. Strict Data Protection: No mobile information will be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

4. Opting Out

You may opt out of communications at any time. To stop receiving text messages, simply reply STOP to any message you receive. For emails, you may request removal by contacting the contractor directly.

5. Security

We implement strict security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information when you enter, submit, or access your personal information.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, you may contact us by calling our main line provided on the homepage.
